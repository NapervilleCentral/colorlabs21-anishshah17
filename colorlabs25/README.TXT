PROJECT TITLE: Poster Project
PURPOSE OF PROJECT: Creating a collage of pictures with different transformations
VERSION or DATE: 5/14/2026
AUTHORS: Anish Shah

To create this poster, I first decided an image that would best suit this collage. I decided on this Lollapalooza picture that I took because I thought that it would look good with the different transformations for this poster. 

For my first transformation, I reflected the picture vertically and made it seem like a mirror was reflecting the two sides of the image down the middle. I really enjoyed this transformation.

For my second transformation, I used the posterize effect and made it seem like a drawing. I also really enjoyed this effect, and how it blurred the colors (by rounding color ranges) and made it seem like it was taken on a digital camera from the 2000s.

For my third transformation, I used the sepia effect, which actually turned out to be more of a grayscale effect, instead of the deep yellows and browns. I think this is due to the neon colors and dark background due to the nighttime. 

For my fourth transformation, I used the recursion effect. I scaled the image at the same ratio subsequently, so that it looked like it was an infinite mirror effect as it kept getting smaller. When I first did this effect, since the background is so dark, all the images blended together with one another. To fix this, I tried negating the image, alternating between negative and normal every image, but the whites from the negated image overpowered the images under it. To fix this, I tinted every other image with "warmer colors" to distinguish each image from one another as they got smaller.

For my fifth transformation, I used the negate effect, which basically flipped the standard image from normal contrast to high contrast. The most significant change was the dark background to a white background, and the colors of the neon letters in the "Lollapalooza" lettering. 

For my sixth, seventh, and eighth transformation, I used the colorReplacement method to replace color ranges in my original image with purple (sixth transformation), navy blue (seventh transformation), and hot pink (eighth transformation). The neon lights in the "Lollapalooza" sign still stand out, because those color ranges are very specific compared to the dark sky or the overall color of the people walking past the sign.
